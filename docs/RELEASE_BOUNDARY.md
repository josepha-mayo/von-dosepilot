The owner authorized a public repository under MIT on 29 September 2026. Original software, project documentation, fictional fixtures and aggregate findings are included.

Biological source workbooks, private metadata templates, private prepared arrays, trained biological weights, patient-level outputs, credentials, font binaries and administrative records are excluded. Do not publish a private full-checkpoint ZIP.

The MIT license does not extend to third-party source data.
