# Protected Lib2 exact-shared-22 attempt: incomplete, no score

30 September 2026.

DosePilot attempted one separately frozen evaluation on the historically reserved Lib2 remainder. The study was intentionally narrower than the 24-head submitted procedure because Gedatolisib and Palbociclib do not have compatible target support under the unchanged Lib2 grid. It therefore asked only whether the **22 unchanged, exactly supported R13 heads** could transfer without refitting.

## Frozen design

The historical reserved frame contained 61 PDO records from 31 whole patients. The candidate used the previously fitted R13 coefficients, scalers and intercepts unchanged. Removing only the two unsupported heads left 58 original selected dose-level measurements per deployment alternative.

A constant-tail log-dose interpolation comparator was independently optimized using Lib1 TRAIN only and the exact cross-library dose identities. It received the same 58-readout budget. Both procedures' paid observations were subsets of the same 22-target endpoint-support cells.

The study authorized at most 19,642 selected endpoint response cells. It prohibited raw signal, bridge responses, genotype, clinical outcomes, nearest-dose relabeling, target substitution, imputation and post-result scope changes.

## What happened

The one-shot numerical importer stopped on a required response that could not be converted to a finite numeric value. The failure occurred **before any prediction vector or efficacy metric was constructed**.

By the frozen access ledger, up to 4,703 selected response cells may have been accessed before termination, spanning 15 PDO records from nine whole patients. Forty-six planned PDO records were not reached, including 45 records belonging to 22 wholly unreached patients.

The protocol's rule was explicit: any missing, nonnumeric or nonfinite required response makes the primary study incomplete. Therefore:

**Status: INCOMPLETE_NO_EFFICACY_SCORE.**

There is no repaired complete-case score, no dropped-patient score, no narrowed-target score, and no retry. The remaining protected records are not being consumed to rescue this hypothesis.

## Interpretation

This outcome is **not a model loss**. It is also not successful validation. The exact22 transfer hypothesis simply did not produce a scorable result under its frozen input contract.

That distinction matters. A method can have strong retrospective development numbers and still fail an external evaluation pipeline because the source assay cannot satisfy the declared endpoint without ad hoc repair. Treating the import failure as a bad prediction would be false; silently changing the denominator after the failure would be worse.

The submitted R13 model, its development MSE and all previously reported external-study results are unchanged.
