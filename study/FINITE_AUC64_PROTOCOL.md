# Finite-observation anchored AUC reconstruction

## Objective and contract
Aim to halve the retained 64-well equal-patient, full24 MSE from 0.001042745722096212 to <=0.000521372861048106, without worsening p90 patient RMSE 0.037419695944064885. Retain original 119 Lib1 samples / 59 whole patients / 24 raw normalized AUC targets; 64 distinct eligible native concentrations, 32 physical wells per plate. A and B remain alternative deployments, never an inference ensemble. No extra standard controls, external responses, patient peers, changed labels or expanded dose menu.

## New structural question
The endpoint is the exact finite-grid linear functional of 416 historical physical treatment values, not a smooth latent drug potency. A purchased cell's contribution is already known. Test separating this exact contribution from the unmeasured remainder, rather than estimating the entire endpoint as a free regression output. The complete historical grid may train models only within fitting partitions. Only the original 328 eligible physical cells can be chosen, and inference still obtains exactly 64 of them.

Arm 1 is the unchanged operating ridge0.01 plus bandwidth0.7 residual model, required to reproduce MSE0.0010582750420801538. Arm 2 subtracts the exact purchased AUC contribution from fitting labels, fits the same own-drug ridge0.01 to the unknown remainder, then adds the exact contribution back. It is a prior-centered regression control, not a new label.

Arms 3-5 fit an explicitly smooth, two-plate covariance prior to complete fitting curves. For each drug, normalize log concentration to [0,1]. Five PSD dose kernels: constant, centered-linear outer product, and RBF with lengthscales0.25/0.5/1.0. Use five shared-across-plate components, five same-plate-only components and one independent-well component, totaling11 nonnegative variance coefficients. Means and covariance moments use exchangeable two-plate augmentation and equal whole-patient weights. Normalize each drug covariance by max(mean variance,0.05^2). Fit local coefficients by nonnegative least squares, and pooled coefficients across drugs with equal per-drug Frobenius weight. Predeclared pooling strengths0,0.5,1 blend local and pooled coefficients. Add 1e-6 times the drug variance scale to the covariance diagonal for numerical definiteness. There is no outcome-tuned basis, noise floor or lengthscale search.

Predict the unknown part by Gaussian conditioning and add the purchased-cell quadrature terms exactly. For an observation set S, beta=Q_S+C_SS^-1 C_SU Q_U and intercept=mu^T Q-mu_S^T beta. The hypothetical all-observed limit returns the raw finite-grid AUC exactly. This is not a claim that technical noise and biology are identified, nor a universal Gaussian data model. Previous full-curve and hierarchical empirical-covariance studies used different covariance structures; this tests a dose-smooth nonnegative covariance decomposition and an observed-value anchor.

For every arm, add the unchanged bandwidth0.7 additive residual kernel with the existing ten spectral options. Primary nested procedure chooses one of50 configurations globally in three inner patient folds within each of five original outer patient folds. Also report each fixed base with inner-only spectral choice. Rebuild acquisition, covariance coefficients, scaling and every predictor inside fitting partitions. No outer-fold or target splicing and no averaging differently purchased panels. The repeatedly inspected Lib1 population remains adaptive development, not independent biological validation.
