# Standard-control transfer across measurement-cost tiers

Frozen specification, 8 October 2026. Primary remains <=0.000521372861048106 MSE and p90<=0.037419695944064885 using 64 treatment wells. Secondary investigates 120/124/128-well accuracy tiers. Higher treatment counts are explicitly NOT same-budget gains versus64. The preceding query-local study is preserved unchanged; no frozen menu from that study is edited after seeing its results.

Hypothesis: the query-local and higher-cost models do not yet use the standard-control quality information present in the retained64 research pipeline. Fitting a shared error correction plus a rank1 target-deviation correction could recover remaining error without adding treatment wells. This is a transfer of established internal calibration ingredients, not a newly invented algorithm.

Base procedure: reproduce the preceding 70-option query-local model selection separately for each of budgets64/120/124/128. Three whole-patient inner folds inside each original five-fold outer partition. Every base panel and model is fit only on its fitting patients. Select one base/spectral option per budget from inner MSE. The inner out-of-fold residuals from that selected option are the fitting targets for the new calibrator; no outer-held patient contributes to its fit. Selection and residual fitting share the outer training data, so only the outer evaluation is used for reported accuracy. This data has been repeatedly studied and remains adaptive development.

Standard controls: use exactly ten previously derived per-sample public TRAIN summaries, with sample IDs matched exactly to the119 authenticated Lib1 samples. A uses the7-column basis [range mean,half-difference,mean squared,negative-logmedian mean/half-difference,positive-logmedian mean/half-difference]. B adds negative and positive control CV mean/half-difference (11 columns). No treatment-response feature is added. These controls are nevertheless required measurements: the new higher-cost model is NOT a treatment-values-only interface, and their laboratory acquisition cost is not claimed to be zero or measured here. They are the same declared standard-control class used by retained64.

Fit each orientation separately on its outer-training residuals. Decompose each residual row into its mean across24 targets and target deviations. Patient-balanced ridge has lambda=max(1e-12,0.125*trace(feature covariance)/feature dimension), centered features and unpenalized intercept. Fit the common scalar, and fit a24-output deviation map then truncate its coefficient matrix to rank1. Preserve the fitted deviation intercept. No target/fold splicing or post-result parameter changes.

Four predeclared arms: uncalibrated base; asymmetric correction (A1/9,B1/3 for both common and deviation); balanced (1/3 for both components and both orientations); PRIMARY full_common (full common scalar correction with A1/9 and B1/3 target-deviation correction). There is no outcome-based selection between these calibration arms. All are reported, and PRIMARY is named before results. The uncalibrated arm must reproduce the previous query-local results exactly.

The physical layout remains one separately purchased A or B alternative, with exactly the declared treatment-well budget split equally across plates, 24 unchanged raw log-dose AUC endpoints, original eligible dose menu. Never average A/B predictions. All119 samples/59 whole patients remain in the denominator. No Protected22/Lib2, external response training, patient-ID features, clinical dosing claims, official scores or automated submission/replacement.

Synthetic checks and complete source/input hashes precede fitting. Save all outer numeric models, controls and purchased values, and sample/patient membership. Independently replay with a separate local weighted-regression solver and direct calibration algebra. Rebuild outerfold0 after changing its excluded treatment responses, labels and control summaries, while keeping the actual query inputs unchanged; fitted states, options and predictions must agree. Check the archived base predictions and whole-patient metrics.

Primary64 promotion screen: strictly lower MSE,>=30 patient wins,5/5 favorable folds,p90 nonworse versus retained64 and its uncalibrated base. Half-error requires the absolute threshold too. Higher-cost options require transparent cost/accuracy comparison; no silent replacement of the existing64 or128 models. Preserve every result, positive or negative. Any release remains research-only pending independent biological confirmation.
