# Hierarchical mean/contrast conditional reconstruction, 8 October 2026

## Objective and novelty relative to previous local work
Halve retained 64-well patient-balanced 24-target MSE from 0.001042745722096212 to <=0.000521372861048106, with p90 patient RMSE <=0.037419695944064885. No extra treatment measurements, no target changes. The earlier full-curve covariance study modeled raw plate cells jointly. This study instead models historical two-plate MEAN curves and paired CONTRAST curves separately, with independent global and drug-local components, then analytically conditions their joint distribution on exactly one purchased 64-cell layout. These latent components are statistical summaries; neither is asserted to identify pure biology or pure technical noise.

For each fitting patient, M=(p1+p2)/2 and D=(p1-p2)/2. A purchased cell is M_j+s_j D_j with s=+1 for p1 and -1 for p2. The unchanged original response is W^T M for the exact full-grid quadrature W. Means and shared per-dose scales are estimated on fitting rows only. Each latent covariance uses a global low-rank factor plus full within-drug residual covariance, shrunk 10% toward its diagonal. The following six configurations are fixed before outcomes: (mean_rank,contrast_rank,cross_dependence)=(0,0,0),(4,0,0),(0,2,0),(4,2,0),(12,2,0),(4,2,0.25). Cross-dependence is a convex mixture with the complete empirical mean/contrast joint covariance, preserving PSD. A fixed 0.01 variance-relative numerical/observation ridge is added to the purchased covariance before conditioning.
